# Product

**Who it is for.** Owners and front desks of small dental and vet practices in South Florida. An empty chair is an hour of the day nobody gets paid for.

**The problem.** Patients forget, or don't come, and nobody follows up. The slot stays empty, and the front desk is busy with the people who did arrive.

**The fix (TEKGUYZ package C).** Patients get a reminder before a visit. A no-show gets a new time offered by text, or a call from the front desk. The open slot goes to the short-notice waitlist. After a visit, a happy patient is asked once for a review, and an unhappy one reaches the team privately.

**This repo** is the landing page and the one-click demo that shows that fix end to end, with made-up data. It is not a product a practice logs into. A real setup is built for the practice's own scheduling system, and clinic privacy rules are still open (`tekguyz-one` `docs/KNOWN_GAPS.md`).

**Voice.** Plain, calm, honest. No prices, no statistics, no setup-time claims, no phone numbers. Never say it is already running for anyone.
