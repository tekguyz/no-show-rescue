# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| No-Show Rescue | The product: reminds patients before a visit, offers a new time after a no-show, fills the open slot from a waitlist, then asks for a review. TEKGUYZ package C. |
| Sample Business | Pinewhistle Dental, the made-up dental practice the demo plays. Not a real practice. |
| Patient | The person with the visit. In the demo, the visitor plays Lena. |
| Front desk | The person at the practice who sees the schedule. In the demo, Rosa. |
| Reminder | The text before a visit. The patient can confirm. |
| No-show | A visit the patient did not come to. 15 minutes after the time, in the demo. |
| Waitlist | Patients who asked for an earlier visit. Texted when a slot opens; the first to reply takes it. |
| Run | One visitor's trip through the demo, from the reminder to the review (`lib/demo/engine.ts`). |
| What would go out | The list of every text the system would send. In the demo, none is sent. |
